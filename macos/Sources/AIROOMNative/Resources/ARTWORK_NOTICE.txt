./Scripts/fetch-original-assets.sh can re-download these files from the pinned
upstream commit and verify their Git blob hashes.

The *-mask.png files are subject masks derived from the portraits above (an adaptation,
under the same CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license). They let the relief card faces show without
loading Vision at launch. After a portrait changes, regenerate them from macos/ with:
  swift Scripts/bake-portrait-masks.swift
A mask whose shape no longer matches its portrait is ignored, and the app falls back to
making the cut-out with Vision.
